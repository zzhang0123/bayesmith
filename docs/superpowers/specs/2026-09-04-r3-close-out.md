### G5 — the random acceptance ledger

This is the gate that is this page rather than a test, and it is the one the
plan asked to be computed rather than argued. §0.4 froze **one** rate,
`ALPHA = 0.05`, **per check**; everything below it is arithmetic.
[`docs/evaluation.md`](../../evaluation.md) states the consequence and this
section counts it.

**Two kinds of row, and the difference matters more than the rate.**

* **Pinned.** The seed is fixed and the statistic is a *count* — a rank among
  ranks, or a tail mass over 2000 draws. Counts do not move at rounding
  scale; the amortized record measured that directly (nudging every posterior
  draw by 1e-7 and 1e-6 changed **0 of 300** ranks, and D and p to four
  digits were unchanged). For these rows the expected false-positive count on
  this platform is **0, and the reason is the seed, not the rate.** What is
  worth reading is the **margin** to the band edge.
* **Re-drawn on another platform.** A NUTS trajectory is chaotic: one ULP in
  a gradient sends the chain elsewhere, which is a genuine re-draw from the
  null. For those the declared level *is* the fire rate, and the cell is
  written as rung (c) with a loud skip.
