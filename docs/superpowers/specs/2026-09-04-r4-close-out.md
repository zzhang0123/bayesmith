## What the adversarial reviews found, and why that is the headline

Three reviews ran, each in an isolated worktree, each instructed to build the
bypass and run it rather than to read the code. They did not polish the batch;
they **blocked** it twice.

| review | verdict | what it found |
|---|---|---|
| Task 1 | six required changes | a premise guard defeated four ways, with one call site in `src/` **already** outside it; a `row += width` deletion invisible because the fixture's unreached node sorted last; an untested branch where a mutant was wrong by 0.349 nats with the fast layer green |
| Task 2 | twelve required changes | two crashes; two docstring claims that were false; seven mutation survivors, all because every fixture had one latent, one observation, one scalar sigma |
| Task 4 | **BLOCK** | thirty-one stock priors misclassified; six of this package's own fixtures crashing; twenty of thirty-five mutations surviving |

**The Task 4 review is the one that matters most.** `dist.HalfCauchy(1.0)` —
the standard weakly-informative scale prior — was reported IMPROPER. So were
`Cauchy`, `StudentT`, `Gamma(0.5,1)`, `Pareto`, `LogNormal`, `InverseGamma`.
The verdict depended on the **units**: the same flat improper prior on a length
was IMPROPER in metres and PROPER in nanometres, because the growth test's
`max(|mass|, 1.0)` floor cannot be tripped by a mass of 1e-8 however fast it
doubles.

**And the plan contained a stop-rule for exactly that, which was not run.**
§4.3: *"Record how many of `tests/exact/models.py`'s shipped fixtures the
predicate refuses. If it refuses a fixture this package ships, stop and rule
before widening."* Six crashed. The measurement was performed only after the
review demanded it, and it is now a standing test rather than a one-off — a
measurement performed once is a measurement that goes stale.

Two of the twelve Task 2 findings deserve naming separately because they are
about prose rather than code:

* **"The third and fourth terms cancel exactly"** was false. They sum to
  `−Σ log s`, which is zero only at unit width — and the same commit contained
  a test asserting precisely that. A docstring contradicting a test in its own
  batch is the sentence the next implementer copies, which is how a
  `logdet(F_bb)` sign error survived a whole release here.
* **"Two derivations that share no linear algebra"** was false. The assembler
  and the square-root route share `unchecked_operator`, `precision_at`,
  `dense_operator` and `observed_descendants`. The review priced it: scaling
  `dense_operator`'s return by 1.03 leaves the cross-route test **completely
  blind** — 5 passed, exit 0 — while the hand derivation fails five and an
  independent quadrature fails fourteen.

## D107's form changed because the measurement said so

The plan derived the dense-agreement band as `C · n · eps · κ₂` from backward
stability of the QR route. Over a 60-cell grid the worst cell is
**well-conditioned** — `k=2, n=4, prior_std=0.05`, `κ₂ = 1.04` — where the
bound is `9.2e-16` and the gap is `1.2e-14`: about thirteen ULP of a log
evidence whose *magnitude* is 10. Conditioning explains none of it, because a
sum of logs carries absolute error proportional to its largest term.

| form | worst C | spread (max/median) |
|---|---:|---:|
| `n eps κ₂` | 13.462 | 694× |
| `n eps \|logZ\|` | 164.585 | 442× |
| `n eps (\|logZ\| + log κ₂)` | 106.900 | 415× |
| **`n eps max(κ₂, \|logZ\|)`** | **1.134** | **60×** |

The winner is the only one whose coefficient is `O(1)`, which is what a
backward-stability argument predicts of a correct form; a form needing a
coefficient of 165 is a form doing the wrong thing with a fudge factor on top.

**Both platforms measure `C = 1.1336`.** macOS/Accelerate and `linux/amd64`
with `OPENBLAS_CORETYPE=ZEN` (numpy 2.5.2, scipy-openblas, QEMU TCG,
`fma avx avx2`, no `avx512f`) agree to five digits — ratio 1.0, well inside the
factor-of-four rule §0.8 set. Given that platform divergence cost this project
four release tags, exact agreement is the outcome worth recording.

## Disclosed limits

**1. One mutant survives the assembly's suite, and it cannot be killed.**
`log_evidence = float(sum(terms.values()))` passes all 25 tests. When every
term is right the two expressions are the same number — here they differ by one
ULP — and asserting they differ bitwise would pin one machine's arithmetic,
which is the fixture class that burned four tags. Priced by compound mutation:
`M5 + drop the prior normaliser` fails the same 10 tests as that fault alone,
`M5 + flip the determinant` the same 11, `M5 + skip the descendant filter` the
same 1. The per-term grading is what catches those, whichever way the total is
spelled.

**2. Two boundary-grid parameters survive mutation by design.** Halving the
prior audit's base resolution changes no verdict, because the convergence check
compares the rule against a refinement of itself: a coarser rule abstains
sooner and never answers differently. The suite asserts that property rather
than pinning 24 and 2, which would go red on changes the design calls free.

**3. R4's admitted class is one structure family.** Whole-graph-exact
linear-Gaussian, `method == "gcr"`. Campaign evidence, chain evidence and a
discrete evidence ROUTE are all outside it. Task 9 records what the existing
discrete enumeration is — `log p(θ, y)`, not `log p(y)`, because it sums
`log_joint` over assignments and `log_joint` carries the continuous latents'
priors — so that a later release opening that route starts from a measured
statement rather than from the function's name.

**4. Correlated observation noise is refused, and it has a perfectly good exact
evidence.** `compress` reads it through `Precision` with no special case. The
dense decomposition has no independent oracle for it, and §9.1 does not admit a
number whose only check is the route that produced it, so the class is refused
rather than shipped ungated. That is a narrower coverage domain than the
arithmetic supports, deliberately.

## Divergences from the plan, written back

* **`evidence_base_measure_undeclared` was declared and then removed.** It was
  a premise for a log-space graph with an unrecorded change of variables, and
  nothing could raise it: a graph reaches log space only through the `log-gcr`
  route, already outside the admitted class, so the structure gate refuses it
  first. Both directions of the vocabulary test passed it — it had a remedy,
  and no fixture named it — because neither asked whether anything CAN name it.
  A reachability test now does, and it immediately found a second premise the
  fixtures could not produce.
* **`evidence_requires_x64` was not in the plan.** It appeared during execution:
  the same assembly agrees with a dense analytic value to 3.4e-07 at float32
  and 8.9e-16 at float64, and an evidence made of absolute constants cannot be
  carried by the first.
* **D109 and D110 were reserved with "expected consumption 0" and were
  consumed.** Both are the prior audit's — the convergent-increment ratio and
  the normalisation tolerance — and both appeared during the Task 4 rework
  rather than in the plan. Red line 8 says a third number stops the work and it
  did: they are registered with boundary grids before this page was written.
  **D108 was reserved and not consumed**: the prior-scale domain became a
  `NotImplementedError` where the width is read, not a registered threshold.
* **Task 7 records the log-space Jacobian and does not fold it into an
  evidence.** The refusal it was written to lift turned out to be the structure
  gate's, so the field is recorded for the release that admits `log-gcr`, and
  the transformed graph's own `log_joint` is asserted bitwise unchanged.

## Two live defects were found while planning, and both are repaired

Neither was an evidence concern; both were found by reading the code an
evidence would have to build on.

* **`dispatch/collapse.py` counted an observed node twice.**
  `marginal_log_density` compressed every node in `block.data` while
  `collapse_graph` absorbed only `observed_descendants`, so an observation the
  exact block does not reach entered the marginal term AND stayed an explicit
  likelihood in the reduced graph. Measured at `fb1c21f`: a surplus of
  `-2.9155099456713867`, which is `log p(e|tau)` to better than 1e-9. A
  likelihood counted twice narrows the posterior by √2, silently. The arm is
  opt-in and every fixture in `test_collapse.py` had one observed node.
* **`marginal/campaign.py:106` collapses an epoch-varying per-epoch prior** to
  the first epoch's value. A 4-epoch campaign declaring `tau = [0.5, 1, 2, 4]`
  returns `-2.944577` where the dense truth is `-5.822657`.

  **R4 does not repair it, and "the evidence layer refuses the campaign family"
  is not a mitigation for anyone else.** `compress_campaign` is in
  `bayesmith.marginal.__all__`; a direct caller of the marginal layer reaches
  this today and R4's refusal does nothing for them. What R4 buys is that no
  evidence is assembled on top of it. Repairing it means deciding what a
  heterogeneous per-epoch prior means for the vectorised fold — the comment at
  `campaign.py:104-105` acknowledges the limitation and names no ledger row —
  and that is a feature with its own oracle, not a line to change here. **Owner
  decision.**

  > **RESOLVED 2026-09-04, after this document was written — repaired, not
  > refused.** The judgement above ("a feature with its own oracle, not a line
  > to change here") was refuted by measurement, which is why the resolution
  > sits on the line that asked rather than in a later document. The
  > information was never missing: `gaussian_parts` already returned the whole
  > per-epoch vector, `nuisance_prior` already broadcast a per-component std,
  > and `one_epoch` was already `vmap`ped, so the prior only had to stop being
  > a closed-over scalar and become a mapped argument — 61 lines, in `895e181`.
  > The owner ruled to land it. `8bd3806` then added the assertions an
  > adversarial review showed were missing: the repair's own tests varied the
  > declared WIDTH only, so a mutant collapsing the declared MEAN passed the
  > entire fast layer while sitting 4.221 nats from the dense truth.
  >
  > Two of this bullet's own particulars are stale and left standing as the
  > record they are: the comment at `campaign.py:104-105` no longer exists, and
  > the figures `-2.944577 / -5.822657` are this document's, taken at a
  > different evaluation point from the `-3.086436 / -5.838560` pinned in
  > `tests/marginal/test_campaign_per_epoch_prior.py`. Take numbers from the
  > test.
  >
  > **What is NOT repaired**, and is disclosed rather than refused: a declared
  > width far below the epoch's noise degrades silently — rel err 5.7e-2 at
  > `1e-16`, and `-inf` at `1e-300` with no refusal. It pre-dates the repair
  > (the homogeneous all-`1e-300` campaign is `-inf` at both commits); the
  > repair makes it reachable from any of the E slots rather than only the
  > first. Refusing below a floor would change published behaviour for callers
  > whose `1e-12` is usable today. **Owner decision, still open.**

* **A multi-dimensional observed node crashed the collapse arm** with
  `Incompatible shapes for broadcasting: shapes=[(3, 2), (6,), ()]` out of
  `jnp.where`, because the arm ravels an observation's data and leaves its
  covariance the declared shape. Pre-dates R4 — it reproduces with a single
  observed node — and R4 does not lift the limitation. It now refuses by name,
  the same treatment the masked observation and the unreached block get.

  This one was found by the Task 2 review, declared out of that task's scope,
  and then **not written down anywhere** until the batch was otherwise
  finished. A defect nobody records is a defect found twice.

- R5 in any form: no nested sampling, no BlackJAX, no JAXNS, no
  `CompiledEvidenceProblem`.

  three of `chain.py`'s constants are unconvicted by deletion.

- A discrete evidence route. What exists is a record of what the enumeration is.
