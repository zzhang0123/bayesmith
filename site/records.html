Research records and design sources

Historical evidence is useful when its date, subject and authority are visible.

Document status

The repository's document index separates the single normative design, current module specifications, decision homes, active plans, records and superseded documents. A dated record is evidence about the run it describes; it is not a current API guarantee.

The user-facing guides here explain current intended use. Internal decisions stay at their established home. Follow source links for audit detail rather than treating every old table as a live roadmap.
