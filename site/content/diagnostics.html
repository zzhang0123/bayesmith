A successful run, a reliable estimator and an adequate model are different claims.

Ask the right diagnostic question

QuestionEvidence
Did the computation complete?Run termination, errors, elapsed work and budget.
Does the sample support estimation?Chain behavior, ESS, R-hat, Monte Carlo error, divergences, weight concentration where relevant.
Can the data determine the parameters?Identifiability, curvature and prior-sensitivity diagnostics.
Does the model reproduce specified features?Prior/posterior predictive discrepancy reports and held-out prediction.
Does an inference route calibrate uncertainty?Simulation-based calibration across independently simulated problems.

Read both report axes

An EvaluationReport separates applicability (APPLICABLE, INAPPLICABLE, UNVERIFIABLE) from conclusion (PASS, FAIL, ABSTAIN). Only applicable checks can pass or fail. Missing inputs, a missing optional backend or unsupported noise must not become a statistical PASS.

ApplicabilityAllowed conclusion
APPLICABLEPASS, FAIL or ABSTAIN
INAPPLICABLEABSTAIN
UNVERIFIABLEABSTAIN

A quality gate aggregates reports under a declared policy. The evaluation layer reads completed results and existing diagnostic verdicts; it does not alter the posterior, choose the next sampler or invent a second numerical threshold.

Select and interpret checks

{{api:bayesmith.evaluation:check_posterior}} is the orchestration entry point. Individual checks include posterior/prior prediction, held-out scores, optional ArviZ PSIS-LOO, SBC, identifiability and prior sensitivity. Observation grouping, draw floors and replicate budgets are part of applicability.

The declared false-positive rate is ALPHA = 0.05. Do not tune discrepancies, seeds or tolerances after seeing whether the model passes. Record which statistics were actually checked.

A PASS has a scope

A predictive PASS bounds the discrepancies evaluated, not all possible model errors. SBC can be satisfied by prior draws that ignore data; it is not evidence of useful posterior learning. Good importance-weight ESS cannot discover an unseen mode.

Use complementary checks and independent oracles. Experimental primitives need separate overlap and truncation-validity assessments.
