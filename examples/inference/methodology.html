bayesmith is a Bayesian compilation layer that sits between the model declaration and the numerical backend. It records a model written as operators into an explicit, inspectable graph, discovers and verifies the graph's structure, eliminates exactly whatever admits a closed form, picks an execution backend for what remains, and hands back results that carry their applicability conditions, diagnostics and provenance. It is not another probabilistic programming language, and not a collection of samplers.bayesmith 是位于模型声明与数值后端之间的贝叶斯编译层。它将以算子形式编写的模型记录为显式、可检查的图，发现并验证图的结构，对具有闭式解的部分进行精确消元，为剩余部分选择执行后端，并返回附带适用条件、诊断和来源记录的结果。它不是另一种概率编程语言，也不是采样器的集合。
